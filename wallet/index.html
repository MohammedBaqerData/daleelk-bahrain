Your GCC Wallet

One wallet for travelling the Gulf. Pay like a local in six GCC currencies, discover tourism offers near you, and earn points that work across borders. Built with NBB.

Open the clickable prototype

📊 Pitch deck (PDF)

  • Six currencies, one wallet: BHD, SAR, AED, QAR, KWD and OMR, with trip mode when you cross a border
  • Pay like a local: scan and pay at partner shops with no foreign card fees
  • Tourism offers near you: festivals, heritage sites, hotels and shops, such as Muharraq Nights
  • Points that cross borders: a GCC passport that rewards you for exploring
  • Partner view: tourism bodies and shops see visitors reached and offers used, as anonymous totals
